Organization visualization guidelines, version 1.0.0 (fictional workshop policy).

Scope: one integration-level component/data-flow diagram in "Architecture diagram".
Show the integration, its evidenced external participants and relevant data stores.
Keep internal functions and retry loops out unless necessary to explain architecture.
Do not depict the documentation pipeline or Foundry hosting infrastructure.

Prefer left-to-right (LR) flow; top-to-bottom (TB) is allowed for readability.
Use stable IDs n1, n2, etc., and retain existing IDs/order when they fit the schema.
Use concise, descriptive ASCII labels (80 characters maximum).
Allowed punctuation in labels: spaces, period, comma, colon, parentheses, slash,
plus, underscore and hyphen. No markup, links, directives or line breaks.

Node kinds and rendered shapes:
- component: rectangle, for the integration and its components.
- external: rounded rectangle, for external systems and participants.
- store: cylinder, only for a data store actually evidenced in the sources.
Label every connection with an evidenced interaction, data item or protocol.
Arrows show the supported direction of interaction or data transfer.
Do not guess a protocol or imply a bidirectional connection without evidence.

Maximum 12 nodes and 24 connections; simpler is better. Do not add disconnected
decorative nodes. Do not reorganize a valid existing diagram merely for aesthetics.
The host renders this graph as conservative Azure DevOps-compatible Mermaid graph
syntax. No custom colors, themes, icons, HTML, click handlers or Mermaid directives.
Semantic consistency and legibility take precedence over branding.
